Your details

When you request The Cold Sales Kit, Kairo Marketing saves your name, phone number and email, plus the page and campaign you came from. We use this to record your request and provide kit access.

Details are stored privately in Kairo’s Vercel project. The form is processed on Vercel. Your details are not added to the public GitHub repository. This form does not automatically subscribe you to marketing messages.

You can ask Kairo to remove or correct your details by contacting Tian through his official channel or your existing Kairo contact.

The kit download is hosted on GitHub. The Poppins font is bundled with this page. Those services have their own privacy settings and policies.
